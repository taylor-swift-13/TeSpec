\subsection{Consistency with verified specifications}
\label{sec:mode-correspondence}
Testing does not require prior verification. The following property instead
connects test results to a completed modular proof.
Let $A_\ell$ be the assertion established at point $\ell$ by that proof.
Verification covers safety, contracts and all selected assertions and
invariants under the same interpretation $\Gamma$ used for testing.
External contracts remain explicit environment assumptions.

At a summary boundary, the verified continuation accepts every post-state
satisfying the full relational contract, including old values and frames.
Both a returning verified body and a legal sample satisfy that relation.
For an original VC $e$, faithful construction must also preserve
$S_e\Rightarrow A_{\ell_e}$ and its original target $Q_e$; verification
supplies $A_{\ell_e}\Rightarrow Q_e$. This source inclusion is essential:
truth at one sampled state alone does not imply validity of a whole VC.

\begin{theorem}[Consistency with verified specifications]
\label{thm:mode-correspondence}
Assume a complete modular verification as above, legal input instances and
boundary samples, and faithful original-VC construction.
Every finite test prefix in either strategy preserves the verified
assertions, and every original VC is valid.
If both tests complete execution and required coverage from nonempty entry
domains, and every required VC receives a sound completed decision, then
both verdicts are \pass{}. Their states, traces and sample choices may differ.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Induction on steps preserves $A_\ell$: ordinary steps use the verified body
obligations; summary steps use the legal relational postcondition and the
universally verified continuation. Verified assertions imply the selected
checks. For each original VC, compose $S_e\Rightarrow A_{\ell_e}$ with
$A_{\ell_e}\Rightarrow Q_e$. Sound completed decisions classify these VCs
as VALID, and the entry and coverage premises yield \pass{} in both modes.
\end{proof}

The supplement gives the expanded argument; the reference module
\texttt{VerifiedSpecificationTesting.v} mechanizes preservation and the
original-VC-to-verdict bridge. Sampler and VC construction correctness
remain implementation interfaces. Partial correctness does not ensure
termination or completion within a proof budget; unfinished tests remain
UNKNOWN.
