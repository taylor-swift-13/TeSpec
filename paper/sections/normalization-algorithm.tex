\subsection{Joint heap and value normalization}
\label{sec:normalization-algorithm}
\label{sec:normalization}
Symbolic execution retains values outside its current heap formula:
return terms, saved call arguments, entry values, and pending checks.
Normalization must preserve their relationship to the heap.
Projecting away $r$ from $r=2\land\mathit{cell}(p,1)$ can preserve the
existentially closed heap assertion while losing the value of a return term
that still refers to $r$. We therefore normalize an assertion $P$ together
with its live terms $\bar t$. Split the logical environment into immutable
root bindings $\rho$ and auxiliary witnesses $\xi$. Their joint denotation is
\[
\obs_\rho(P,\bar t)(s,\bar v)
 =\exists\xi.\;P(s,\rho,\xi)\land
   \bar v=\operatorname{eval}(\bar t)_{s,\rho,\xi}.
\]
An admitted reduction from $(P,\bar t)$ to $(P',\bar t')$ preserves this
relation pointwise for each $\rho$. Live terms include every value used by
the continuation or a selected check; these uses must depend only on the
preserved state, root bindings, and live values.

\begin{lstlisting}[language={},caption={Normalize the heap and its live values together.},label={lst:normalize}]
Normalize(Gamma, P, liveTerms, budget):
  while budget > 0 and a reduction is admitted:
    resolve the rule in Gamma
    establish its guard from P
    freshen typed witnesses
    replace the complete equivalent expression
    update dependent liveTerms consistently
    simplify exposed computations
    budget := budget - 1
  return (P, liveTerms)
\end{lstlisting}

A guarded rule requires an equation in the selected interpretation,
\[
\Gamma\models g(\bar a)\Rightarrow
 \bigl(H(\bar a)\leftrightarrow\exists\bar w.\,B(\bar a,\bar w)\bigr),
\]
and validity of $g(\bar a)$ in the reached state. An unresolved guard retains
the original application. Unfolding preserves every pure constraint, resource,
alternative, and typed witness. A checked pure consequence may instead be
exposed by $H\leftrightarrow H\land G$, retaining the spatial predicate.

Fresh witnesses cannot capture live variables. Even a syntactically unused
witness of nominal type $T$ carries an existence condition:
\[
(\exists w:T.\,P)\leftrightarrow
(\mathit{inhabited}(T)\land P),\qquad w\notin\mathsf{FV}(P).
\]
Integer and list types have canonical witnesses; arbitrary nominal types
retain the inhabitance obligation. Shared-base offsets and closed list
operations can simplify while addresses remain symbolic. C bounds and
arithmetic safety remain required checks.

\texttt{DefinitionNormalization.v} proves that composing licensed reductions
preserves this joint denotation, nonemptiness, validity, and countermodel
queries. Rule licensing is an explicit premise. Budget exhaustion returns
the residual expression with its meaning preserved.
